Made some progress hunting down rare dacta stuff — a passion at the intersection of lego and retro electronics. Dug up this floppy from the eighties on eBay — a LEGO Control Logo disk. It's a floppy for the British [BBC Micro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Micro) computer.

There aren't many good photos of floppies from that era online, so I stuck this one in an office scanner =)
