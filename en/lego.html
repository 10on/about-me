// notes on this topic

It all started when I saw someone selling Bionicle parts on a classifieds site. Suddenly I remembered how, as a kid, I'd lose pieces and think they were gone forever. I brought my Bionicles from Omsk, a couple of friends saw them and gave me my first Technic set. Then I wanted to build a huge moving excavator — and everything after that is a blur.

Now I've got more than 300 sets and kilograms of pieces — from the present day back to the late sixties. First it was Technic, then trains, and then came... total immersion regardless of theme or release year :)

At some point I wanted to start sharing — first came floating boat builds, then the urge to show how it's done. That's how YouTube and Telegram happened. I keep it up slowly, but with heart.

In 2025 I went to the BuWizz Gathering in Slovenia — a LEGO Technic competition. Honestly, I did really badly. But I want to go again in 2026 — if I get the visa :)
